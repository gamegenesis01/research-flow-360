## The big story

### The Fed holds rates steady and signals one cut next year

The Federal Reserve left its benchmark rate unchanged on Wednesday, as expected. The surprise was in its projections: officials now see one rate cut next year, down from two in September.

The chair said inflation has cooled but "not convincingly enough" in services, and that the job market remains solid. Stocks dipped on the news, then recovered by the close.

> Why it matters: Fewer cuts means borrowing costs stay higher for longer: mortgages, car loans and credit cards are unlikely to get much cheaper soon.

Source: [Federal Reserve statement and projections](https://www.federalreserve.gov/)

## Quick hits

### [Washington] Senate passes a stopgap bill to fund the government into February
The bill avoids a shutdown this month and now goes to the House. Markets had priced in a deal.
Source: [Senate record](https://www.senate.gov/)

### [Earnings] A large chipmaker beats on data-center demand
Revenue from AI data centers rose sharply from a year ago, and guidance came in above forecasts. Shares rose about 6% after hours.
Source: company earnings release

### [Housing] Mortgage rates dip to a three-month low
The average 30-year fixed rate eased for a third straight week, though applications remain weak.
Source: [Freddie Mac weekly survey](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)

### [Energy] Oil slips as U.S. inventories rise
Crude stockpiles grew more than expected last week, pushing prices to a two-week low.
Source: [EIA weekly petroleum report](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/)

### [Media] Streaming ad spending is set to pass cable next year
Advertisers are moving budgets to streaming faster than expected, according to new industry estimates.
